The 2026 Autumn Handicap race will take place instead of regular training on Tuesday 22nd September.
Most of you know the concept by now, but for those that don't ... this is a timed event with a staggered start based on predicted finish times.  The faster you are, the longer you will have to wait to start, and, if I've done my sums right, everyone will finish at about the same time.
The distance is 5k, and the route is the standard mob match one: [https://www.strava.com/segments/30473818](https://www.strava.com/segments/30473818)
The clubhouse will be open from 6.15, with the first runner starting at 7pm.  Please arrive by 6.45 latest to check in, collect a number, and warm up.
We need a few volunteers to make this happen, so please comment if you are able to help out (but don't click "attending" or Paul will put you in the race!).
Results & prizegiving will be shortly after the race, so do stick around.  
Any questions, holler below.
UTM!
