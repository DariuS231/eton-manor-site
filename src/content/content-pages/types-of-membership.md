**Membership fees**

The club membership year runs from April – March.

First Claim Senior – £50 for Membership year (covering EA Membership)
Second Claim Senior – £20
Unwaged (retired / unemployed / students) – £16 (covering EA Membership)
Juniors (under 18) – £2
Associate (non-running member) – £2

**Try us out**

Before you join you are welcome to attend a few sessions to see if it is for you and meet some members of the club. To do this you will need to register ahead of time using the app SPOND. Click on this link to register: [https://group.spond.com/AYQKM](https://group.spond.com/AYQKM)

**How To Join**

Sign-up or renewal is via the Spond App:

```plain
https://club.spond.com/landing/signup/etonmanorac
```

Once the payment has been made, we will complete your
EA registration, and add you to the clubs Spond groups. For more information about membership and how to register to attend the club sessions, please email our Membership Secretary: [membership@eton-manor.com](mailto:membership@eton-manor.com)

**England Athletics**

As a Senior First Claim member we will automatically register you with amateur athletics governing body – England Athletics. EA registration is needed to be able to compete in a number of league races that the club participate in.
As an England Athletics registered athlete, you’ll be part of a nationwide community of athletes and runners whilst also helping us to support your club and the sport we all love.


Your registration fee also gives you access to a range of benefits and services, such as a small discount on all race entries and access to regional / national cross country championships. Once registered you will receive regular emails from EA with information and can find out more information here:

[https://www.englandathletics.org/athletics-and-running/athlete-registration/benefits-of-registration/](https://www.englandathletics.org/athletics-and-running/athlete-registration/benefits-of-registration/)
