Eton Manor AC is the running club for Leyton, Walthamstow, Leytonstone and Hackney Wick, with a youth section offering an athletics programme including running, jumping and throwing.

Eton Manor is the name of an area now part of the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park and is synonymous with a rich history of recreational sport and youth programmes in the area now known as Hackney Wick, Leyton, Walthamstow and Leytonstone.

You can find out more about [our history here](/pages/our-history) and more about [who we are today here](/pages/who-we-are).
