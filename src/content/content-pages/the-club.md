Club activity is made up of training, races and social events across two sections: senior and youth.

Our senior section trains throughout the year with sessions for all abilities, plus more than 30 opportunities each year to race in local leagues, intra-club events and national competitions. Find out more about our [training](/training) and [racing](/pages/racing).

Our youth section trains once a week across a broader range of athletics disciplines, with competition opportunities including termly games events, local league races and annual events such as the London Youth Games and London Mini Marathon. Find out more on the [Youth Section](/pages/youth-section) page.

For more information on all club activities, see the specific pages and if you can’t find what you’re looking for, [just get in touch](mailto:admin@eton-manor.com).
