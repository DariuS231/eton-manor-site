Eton Manor takes the welfare and wellbeing of its members very seriously. It is everyone’s responsibility to ensure the safety and enjoyment of all those who participate in club events. Everyone should feel safe and able to participate in club events without being subject to any form of harassment, bullying, verbal or physical abuse or excessive pressure. We have adopted and abide by the [England Athletics club safeguarding and welfare guidelines](https://www.englandathletics.org/clubs-and-facilities/club-support-services/club-welfare/) and the [UK Athletics Safeguarding Policies and Procedures](https://www.uka.org.uk/governance/safeguarding_2021/)

For advice and support regarding any welfare issues or to raise or report any concerns, please contact our Welfare Officer Christina Long via [welfare@eton-manor.com](mailto:welfare@eton-manor.com)
