The youth section meets during the school term time only from 6:30 till 7:30 on Monday nights at the 📍[Feel Good Centre, 170 Chingford Road E17 5AA](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZdvH8nrZ8me13Ly16).

The session is open to young athletes aging from 8 to 16 years of age, the cost of the session is £1 to be given to the coach at the start of the session. Please note that the Feel Good Centre also charges to use the track which is outside of our control - please check the Better website for more information and how to book in advance.

All sessions are led by qualified EA Athletics coaches and assistants, with  sessions covering running, jumping and throwing as well as some fun games at the end of each school term.

As well as coached sessions on a Monday night there are opportunities to race in local competitions (such as the Chingford league) against other clubs in the area, or at local track meets.

For more information please contact [emacyouth@gmail.com](mailto:emacyouth@gmail.com)
