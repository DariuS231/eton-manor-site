The youth section meets during term time from 6:30 till 7:30 on Monday nights at the Feel Good Centre, 170 Chingford Road E17 5AA.

The session is open to young athletes aging from 8 till 18 years of age, the cost of the session is £1 to be given to the coach at the start of the session (the feel good centre also charges to use the track but this is outside of our control).

All sessions are lead by a qualified EA Athletics coach along with assistants and the sessions covers running, jumping and throwing as well as some fun games at the end of term.

As well as coached sessions on a Monday night there are opportunities to race within the Chingford league against other clubs in the area, or at local track meets

For more information please contact [emacyouth@gmail.com](mailto:emacyouth@gmail.com)
