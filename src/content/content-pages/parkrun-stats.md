At Eton Manor we love parkrun! Our members run regularly at Hackney Marshes, Wanstead Flats, Walthamstow, Mile End & Barking, as well as visiting locations all over the country as parkrun tourists. We love to volunteer too; some of our members are even part of the events teams at Hackney Marshes and at Walthamstow!

As well as 5k times, parkrun kindly provide age-graded percentages. The grading is your 5k time as a percentage of the world record time for your sex and age. This allows you to compare your personal performance against other people’s performances even though they might be a different age and a different sex to you – the higher the score the better the performance.

We keep an annual record of our members best performances. You can view the current scores in this online table – and click on tabs to view results from prior years:
