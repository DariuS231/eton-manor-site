Congratulations to our men’s team on coming home in first place at the 2023 Midsummer relay. Sam, George, Ed & James ran fantastically well to beat a quality Orion Harriers team.

Our ladies team of Lee, Liz, Jess & Jenny fought hard to finish second behind a strong team from Loughton.

The youth team were well represented on the night too. Well done to Matilda, Tom, Sam, Luka & Toby!

It was great to see Tom out supporting us all in his 50th anniversary year with Eton Manor. UTM!
